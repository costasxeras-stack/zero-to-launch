# 0003: Launch inside Spend Lens, not as a new app

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

A new app needs its own import, categories and trust.

## Decision

Build Our Money as a shared space in Spend Lens and sequence it after transfer matching and split payments.

## Tradeoff

- Tied to Spend Lens' growth
- Reuses import and categories, and the invite becomes the growth loop
