# 0002: Personal payments are hidden from the partner

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

Joint-account apps show everything. Some couples keep money personal on purpose.

## Decision

Payments are shared or personal. The partner sees personal payments only as "personal", without place or amount.

## Tradeoff

- Less transparency
- Respects the partly-sharing couple. Interviews will test whether they want it
